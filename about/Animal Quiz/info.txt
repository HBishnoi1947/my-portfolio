Animal Quiz - Kids is the ultimate learning and fun experience for children! Dive into the amazing world of animals with our engaging and educational quiz app. Perfect for curious minds, this app helps kids learn about their favorite creatures in a playful and interactive way.

Key Features :

Picture Quizzes: Can you guess the animal from its picture? Challenge yourself to identify a variety of animals from different habitats.

Fact Quizzes: Learn interesting and fun facts about animals from all over the world. Discover what they eat, where they live, and more!

National Animals: Test your knowledge of geography and wildlife by identifying the national animals of different countries.

Timed Tests: Take a full test to see how much you've learned. Track your progress and try to beat your own high score.

Kid-Friendly Interface: The app features colorful graphics, simple navigation, and a user-friendly design that is easy for kids to use on their own.

Designed to be both entertaining and educational, "Animal Quiz - Kids" makes learning about the animal kingdom a delightful adventure. It's a great tool for parents and teachers to help children build their knowledge in a fun and interactive format. Get ready to explore the wild world of animals!
