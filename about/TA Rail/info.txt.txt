TA Rail is a simple travelling allowance calculation app that helps the user to quickly calculate the travelling allowance (Percentage & Amount) with its in-built calculations based on the input given by the user and gives the output in a submittable pdf format.

Features:
1. Documentation app.
2. Data analyzation with graph.
3. Data saved & secured.
4. No manual calculations.

5. My Profile : Personal detail storage. No need to enter again. If want to update, then can.
a. Name
b. Branch
c. Zone
d. Division
e. Head-Quarter
f. Designation
g. Date of Appointment/ Joining
h. Basic Pay
i. Pay Scale
j. Grade Pay
k. Provident Fund No.
l. Travelling Allowance rate

6. Duty Entry: Create a log of duty at one place
a. Add Train Number, Departure – Arrival time, From-To and Objective of Travel to create an Entry. Edit Log.

7. Preview: A well organised glimpse and view of the Duty Entries for a better on-the-go summary. Delete the monthly data which is not needed.

8. Download PDF: Enter Month and Year of Duty for calculating the Travelling Allowance. Option to select Page Size: A4 and Legal. Options to Save, Share and Print the file.

Note: PDF downloaded is in submittable format and so can directly be submitted to your own office.

9. Contact Us: Feel free to contact the app developer for any complaints or suggestions.

Data Source and Calculation Basis:
This application provides a calculation tool for calculating allowance based on the rules and guidelines as published in the Indian Railway Establishment Code, Chapter 16 - Travelling Allowance Rules. The information and calculation logic are derived from publicly available data from official government sources.

The specific reference document is available at: https://indianrailways.gov.in/railwayboard/uploads/directorate/establishment/estbl-chap16-p01-p50.jsp
